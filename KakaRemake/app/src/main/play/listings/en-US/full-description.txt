🦅 Kaka & Chui: Learn Swahili — The Fun Way for Little Learners! 🐆

Introduce your toddler to Swahili with Kaka & Chui, an interactive educational game for children ages 3–6. Join Kaka (the eagle) and Chui (the leopard) on a learning adventure through the sounds, words, numbers, shapes, and colors of Kiswahili!

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📚 SIX LEARNING MODULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✦ Vowels (Irabu) — Learn the five Swahili vowels A, E, I, O, U with clear pronunciation and fun animations.
✦ Words (Maneno) — Discover everyday Swahili words with beautiful illustrations and native audio.
✦ Numbers 1–10 (Nambari 1–10) — Count from moja to kumi with colorful flash cards and engaging visuals.
✦ Numbers 11–20 (Nambari 11–20) — Advance your counting skills from kumi na moja to ishirini.
✦ Shapes (Maumbo) — Explore circles, squares, triangles, and more in Swahili.
✦ Colors (Rangi) — Learn to identify and name colors in Kiswahili with vibrant, eye-catching cards.

🎮 FOUR ENGAGING GAME MODES
