🦜 Kaka & Chui - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Developer: Game254 Studios

Summary for Parents: Kaka & Chui is designed for children under 5. We do NOT collect personal information from your child. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and Google Play's Families Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal, anonymous information:

  • Learning Progress: Quiz scores, completed lessons, and earned achievements. This is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Anonymous Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g., which modules are most popular). This data is anonymous and cannot identify your child.
  • Crash Reports: If the app crashes, anonymous crash data is sent to help us fix bugs.

We do NOT collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, precise location, device identifiers tied to personal information, or any other personal data.

2. Advertising

Our free version shows limited ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • All ads are child-directed and rated "G" (General audiences)
  • We do NOT show personalized or interest-based ads
  • Ads appear only on the home screen (banner) and between quizzes (interstitial, max once per 5 minutes)
  • Rewarded ads are only accessible through a parental gate
  • Premium subscribers see no ads at all

3. In-App Purchases

All purchases are processed through Google Play Billing and protected by a parental gate (math problem that toddlers cannot solve). We never encourage children to make purchases.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All learning data is stored locally on your device
  • No data is uploaded to external servers (except anonymous analytics)
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: For age-appropriate advertisements (child-directed mode)
  • Google Play Billing: For processing subscription payments
  • Firebase Analytics: For anonymous usage statistics
  • Firebase Crashlytics: For anonymous crash reports

All third-party services are configured for COPPA compliance.

6. Parental Rights

As a parent or guardian, you have the right to:

  • Review your child's learning progress (available in Parent Zone)
  • Delete all app data (uninstall the app or use "Delete Data" in Parent Zone)
  • Control ad preferences (upgrade to premium for ad-free experience)
  • Set daily time limits (available in Parent Zone)

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Kaka & Chui is designed for children under 13 and complies with:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Google Play Families Policy
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requirements for children

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on our Play Store listing.
